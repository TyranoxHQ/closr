Closr by Tyranox Support & Issues

Privacy Policy (Microsoft Store Edition)

Last Updated: November 17, 2026 • Tyranox
Factual Claims Verified by Code Audit:
  1. Closr (Microsoft Store edition) never transmits file contents, folder structures, chat/text messages, or device names to Tyranox or any cloud server.
  2. Closr contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, crash-reporting beacons, or tracking cookies.
  3. In the Microsoft Store build (CLOSR_DISTRIBUTION=store), license-server activation/feedback calls and GitHub update checks are disabled; the app makes no background HTTP requests to Tyranox or GitHub.
  4. All peer discovery (mDNS over UDP port 5353) and transfers (Mutual TLS 1.3 over TCP) stay strictly on your local Wi-Fi/LAN subnet.
  5. Closr Pro (Windows) is sold exclusively through Microsoft Store in-app purchase (Windows.Services.Store.StoreContext). Payment processing is handled by Microsoft; Tyranox never receives your payment details.

1. Overview

Tyranox ("we", "our", "us") builds local-first software. Closr lets you share files, folders, and text between your Windows PC and nearby Android devices over your local Wi-Fi network without creating an account and without uploading your files to a cloud server.

2. Data Handled Locally on Your Device

The Microsoft Store edition stores all app configuration and cryptographic pairing state locally inside Windows packaged app storage (LocalState):

  • Device Name & Type: The display name and device icon you choose are broadcast only on your local Wi-Fi subnet via mDNS so nearby devices can identify your PC.
  • Local LAN IP Addresses: Your local subnet IP address and port (TCP 61734 or ephemeral fallback) are advertised on your LAN via mDNS and cached locally for paired peers in peer_endpoints.json so devices can connect directly without a cloud relay.
  • Cryptographic Identity & Trust Keys: Your device certificate (device_cert.der), DPAPI-encrypted private key (device_key.enc), and paired peer public-key fingerprints (trust_store.json) remain strictly on your device.
  • Files, File Names & Text Transfers: File names, folder structures, file contents, and text messages are transferred peer-to-peer over Mutual TLS 1.3 directly to your chosen recipient device and recorded only in local transfer history. Received files are saved locally (for example, to your Downloads folder) and never pass through Tyranox servers.

3. User Controls & Deleting Your Data

  • Remove Paired Devices: You can unpair or revoke any trusted device in Closr's Trusted Devices settings, which removes its trust key from trust_store.json and blocks future auto-connections.
  • Disable Startup or Context Menu: Launching at sign-in is off by default and only enabled if you opt in; you can turn it off anytime in Closr Settings or Windows Settings (→ Apps → Startup).
  • Delete App Data on Uninstall: Uninstalling Closr from Windows deletes the packaged app and all packaged LocalState data (settings, device certificate/key, trust store, peer endpoints, and local history). Any files you already received and saved to your Downloads folder remain on your PC.

4. Microsoft Store In-App Purchases

If you purchase Closr Pro (Windows) inside the app, the transaction is processed by Microsoft via the Microsoft Store (Windows.Services.Store.StoreContext). Microsoft handles billing, payment methods, receipts, and refunds under Microsoft's Privacy Statement. Closr queries only your local/Store entitlement status and does not receive or store your credit card, billing address, or Microsoft account password.

5. Analytics, Crash Reporting & External Links

Closr includes no crash-reporting SDKs, telemetry pipelines, or usage analytics. The app only opens an external web browser or Microsoft Store page if you explicitly click a link (such as Support, Privacy Policy, or Leave a Review).
